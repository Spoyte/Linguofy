
# Section 2: The Past and the Passive

This section dives deep into the past. You'll learn about reflexive verbs for daily routines and master a second past tense, the imperfect, which is used for descriptions and habitual actions. The highlight of this section is learning to choose between the two past tenses, a key skill for storytelling. We'll also explore ways to form passive sentences.

**Lessons in this section:**

*   **Lesson 1: Reflexive Verbs:** Learn about actions you do to yourself, like `levantarse` (to get up).
*   **Lesson 2: Imperfect Tense (Regular Verbs):** Learn the tense for "used to do" and what "was happening."
*   **Lesson 3: Imperfect Tense (Irregulars):** Master the three and only three irregular verbs in the imperfect.
*   **Lesson 4: Preterite vs. Imperfect:** Learn the fundamental differences between the two past tenses.
*   **Lesson 5: Constructions with "Se":** Discover the impersonal and passive uses of the pronoun `se`.
