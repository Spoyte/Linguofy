
# Lesson 2: Imperfect Tense (Regular Verbs)

**New Words:**
*   hablaba - I used to speak / was speaking
*   comía - I used to eat / was eating
*   vivía - I used to live / was living
*   siempre - always
*   a menudo - often
*   cuando era niño/a - when I was a child

**Grammar:**
The imperfect tense is another past tense. Unlike the preterite (which is for completed actions), the imperfect is used for:
1.  **Habitual or repeated actions in the past** (what you *used to do*).
2.  **Descriptions of people, places, and situations in the past.**
3.  **Actions in progress in the past** (what *was happening*).
4.  **Telling time and age in the past.**

**Regular Verb Endings:**

| Pronoun | -ar Verbs | -er/-ir Verbs |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| yo | **-aba** | **-ía** |
| tú | **-abas** | **-ías** |
| él/ella/ud. | **-aba** | **-ía** |
| nosotros/as | **-ábamos**| **-íamos** |
| vosotros/as | **-abais** | **-íais** |
| ellos/ellas/uds.| **-aban** | **-ían** |

Note the accent marks on all `-er`/`-ir` endings and the `nosotros` form for `-ar`.

Examples:
*   Cuando era niño, **jugaba** en el parque todos los días. (When I was a child, I **played/used to play** in the park every day.)
*   **Hacía** mucho sol. (It **was** very sunny.)
*   Yo **leía** un libro cuando sonó el teléfono. (I **was reading** a book when the phone rang.)
