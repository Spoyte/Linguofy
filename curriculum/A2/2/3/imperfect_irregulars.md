
# Lesson 3: Imperfect Tense (Irregulars)

This lesson is short and sweet! There are only **three** irregular verbs in the imperfect tense.

**Grammar:**

**Conjugations:**

| Pronoun | Ir (to go) | Ser (to be) | Ver (to see) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| yo | **iba** | **era** | **veía** |
| tú | **ibas** | **eras** | **veías** |
| él/ella/ud. | **iba** | **era** | **veía** |
| nosotros/as | **íbamos**| **éramos** | **veíamos** |
| vosotros/as | **ibais** | **erais** | **veíais** |
| ellos/ellas/uds.| **iban** | **eran** | **veían** |

**Key points:**
*   Notice the accent marks on the `nosotros` forms of `ir` and `ser`.
*   Notice the accent marks on all forms of `ver`.
*   That's it! Every other verb is regular in the imperfect.

Examples:
*   Nosotros **íbamos** a la playa cada verano. (We used to go to the beach every summer.)
*   Mi abuelo **era** muy alto. (My grandfather was very tall.)
*   Yo no **veía** bien sin mis gafas. (I didn't see well without my glasses.)
