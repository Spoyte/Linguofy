
# Lesson 4: Preterite vs. Imperfect

This lesson contrasts the two main past tenses. There are no new words, just a focus on usage.

**Grammar Summary:**

Think of the imperfect as setting the **scene** or **background** of a story, while the preterite describes the specific **actions** or **events** that happened.

| Preterite (Action) | Imperfect (Scene) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **S**pecific, completed actions | **H**abitual actions (used to) |
| **I**nterrupting action | **A**ge |
| **M**ain event | **T**ime / **W**eather |
| **B**eginning/End of an event | **E**motion / **D**escription |
| **A**rrivals/Departures | **L**ocation |

A helpful mnemonic:
*   Preterite: **SIMBA**
*   Imperfect: **HATED L**

**Example of Both Tenses Together:**

*   Yo **leía** (imperfect - background action) un libro cuando mi amigo me **llamó** (preterite - interrupting action).
    *   *I was reading a book when my friend called me.*

*   **Hacía** (imperfect - weather description) frío y **llovía** (imperfect - weather description). De repente, **vi** (preterite - main event) un accidente.
    *   *It was cold and it was raining. Suddenly, I saw an accident.*

*   Cuando **era** (imperfect - age/description) niño, **jugué** (preterite - specific event) al fútbol en el campeonato una vez.
    *   *When I was a child, I played football in the championship one time.*
