
# Lesson 1: Reflexive Verbs

**New Words:**
*   levantarse - to get up
*   ducharse - to shower
*   vestirse (e:i) - to get dressed
*   peinarse - to comb one's hair
*   acostarse (o:ue) - to go to bed
*   me, te, se, nos, os, se - reflexive pronouns

**Grammar:**
A reflexive verb is one where the subject performs the action on itself. The verb is conjugated normally, but it is always accompanied by a reflexive pronoun that matches the subject. The infinitive form is recognizable by the "-se" attached to the end.

The reflexive pronouns are:
*   yo -> **me**
*   tú -> **te**
*   él/ella/ud. -> **se**
*   nosotros/as -> **nos**
*   vosotros/as -> **os**
*   ellos/ellas/uds. -> **se**

Placement is the same as other pronouns: before a conjugated verb or attached to an infinitive.

Examples:
*   Yo **me levanto** a las siete. (I get up at seven.)
*   Ella va a **ducharse**. (She is going to shower.)
*   Ellos **se visten** para la fiesta. (They get dressed for the party.) - *Note the stem change.*
