
# Lesson 5: Constructions with "Se"

You've seen "se" used for reflexive verbs and as an indirect object pronoun. Here are two more common uses.

**Grammar:**

**1. The Impersonal "Se"**

This is used to make general statements about what "one" does, what "people" do, or what "you" (in a general sense) do. It's very common in signs and instructions.

The structure is: **se + verb in the 3rd person singular**.

Examples:
*   ¿Cómo **se dice** "book" en español? (How does one say "book" in Spanish?)
*   En España, **se come** muy tarde. (In Spain, people eat very late.)
*   **Se vive** bien aquí. (One lives well here. / The living is good here.)

**2. The Passive "Se"**

This is used to say that something is done, without saying *who* does it. The focus is on the object receiving the action. The verb agrees with the object.

*   If the object is singular, use the **3rd person singular verb**.
*   If the object is plural, use the **3rd person plural verb**.

Examples:
*   **Se vende** coche. (Car for sale. / A car is sold.)
*   **Se venden** coches. (Cars for sale. / Cars are sold.)
*   **Se necesita** ayuda. (Help is needed.)
*   **Se buscan** programadores. (Programmers are sought/wanted.)
