# Lesson 1: Demonstrative Adjectives and Pronouns

**New Words - Demonstrative Adjectives:**
- este/esta - this (near me)
- estos/estas - these (near me)
- ese/esa - that (near you)
- esos/esas - those (near you)
- aquel/aquella - that (far from both)
- aquellos/aquellas - those (far from both)

**New Words - Demonstrative Pronouns:**
- esto - this (thing/idea)
- eso - that (thing/idea)
- aquello - that (distant thing/idea)

**Grammar Rules:**

**As Adjectives (before nouns):**
- Este libro es interesante - This book is interesting
- Esa casa es muy grande - That house is very big
- Aquellos carros son caros - Those cars (over there) are expensive

**As Pronouns (replacing nouns):**
- No me gusta este, prefiero ese - I don't like this one, I prefer that one
- ¿Qué es esto? - What is this?
- Aquello fue muy extraño - That (over there) was very strange

**Distance Concept:**
- **Este/Esta:** Close to the speaker (this/these)
- **Ese/Esa:** Close to the listener (that/those)
- **Aquel/Aquella:** Far from both speaker and listener (that/those over there)

**Practice Examples:**
- ¿Te gusta esta camisa o prefieres esa? - Do you like this shirt or do you prefer that one?
- Estos zapatos son cómodos - These shoes are comfortable
- Aquella montaña es muy alta - That mountain (over there) is very tall
- ¿Qué es eso que tienes en la mano? - What is that you have in your hand?

**Real-World Usage:**
- **Shopping:** "¿Cuánto cuesta este vestido?" "Este cuesta cincuenta euros"
- **Giving directions:** "¿Ve aquella farmacia? Gire a la derecha después de esa"
- **Pointing things out:** "Eso que está comiendo se ve delicioso"

**Common Expressions:**
- En este momento - At this moment
- Por esa razón - For that reason
- Eso es - That's right
- ¿Cómo es eso? - How is that?/What do you mean?
