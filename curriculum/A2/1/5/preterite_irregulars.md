
# Lesson 5: Preterite Tense (Irregulars: Ser, Ir, Dar, Ver)

This lesson covers some of the most common and irregular verbs in the preterite tense. There are no new vocabulary words, only new verb forms.

**Grammar:**
These verbs are highly irregular and must be memorized. The good news is that **Ser** (to be) and **Ir** (to go) have the *exact same* conjugations in the preterite! Context will tell you which verb is being used.

**Conjugations:**

| Pronoun | Ser / Ir | Dar (to give) | Ver (to see) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| yo | **fui** | **di** | **vi** |
| tú | **fuiste** | **diste** | **viste** |
| él/ella/ud. | **fue** | **dio** | **vio** |
| nosotros/as | **fuimos**| **dimos** | **vimos** |
| vosotros/as | **fuisteis**| **disteis**| **visteis**|
| ellos/ellas/uds.| **fueron**| **dieron**| **vieron** |

**Key points:**
*   None of these forms have accent marks.
*   The endings for `dar` and `ver` are very similar to regular `-er`/`-ir` verbs, but without the accents.

Examples:
*   Ayer **fui** al mercado. (Yesterday I **went** to the market.) - *Ir*
*   La película **fue** muy buena. (The movie **was** very good.) - *Ser*
*   **Vi** a tu hermano anoche. (I **saw** your brother last night.) - *Ver*
*   Le **di** el regalo. (I **gave** him the gift.) - *Dar*
