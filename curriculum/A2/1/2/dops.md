
# Lesson 2: Direct Object Pronouns

**New Words:**
*   me - me
*   te - you (informal)
*   lo - him, it, you (formal, m.)
*   la - her, it, you (formal, f.)
*   nos - us
*   os - you (plural, informal, Spain)
*   los - them, you (plural, m.)
*   las - them, you (plural, f.)
*   ver - to see
*   comprar - to buy

**Grammar:**
A direct object is the noun that directly receives the action of the verb. A direct object pronoun (DOP) replaces that noun.
*   *Veo a Juan.* -> **Lo** veo. (I see Juan. -> I see him.)
*   *Compro el libro.* -> **Lo** compro. (I buy the book. -> I buy it.)

Placement rules:
1.  **Before a conjugated verb.**
    *   *¿Tienes mi libro?* -> *Sí, **lo** tengo.* (Yes, I have it.)
2.  **Attached to an infinitive.**
    *   *Quiero ver**lo**.* (I want to see it.)
3.  **Attached to a present participle (-ando/-iendo).**
    *   *Estoy buscándo**lo**.* (I am looking for it.) - *Note: accent mark needed.*

Examples:
*   ¿Comes la manzana? -> Sí, **la** como. (Yes, I eat it.)
*   ¿Ves a tus amigos? -> Sí, **los** veo. (Yes, I see them.)
