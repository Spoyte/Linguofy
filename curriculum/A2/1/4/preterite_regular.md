
# Lesson 4: Preterite Tense (Regular Verbs)

**New Words:**
*   hablé - I spoke
*   comí - I ate
*   viví - I lived
*   ayer - yesterday
*   anoche - last night
*   la semana pasada - last week

**Grammar:**
The preterite tense is a past tense used for completed actions in the past. It describes what *happened* or what someone *did* at a specific, finished point in time.

**Regular Verb Endings:**

| Pronoun | -ar Verbs | -er/-ir Verbs |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| yo | **-é** | **-í** |
| tú | **-aste** | **-iste** |
| él/ella/ud. | **-ó** | **-ió** |
| nosotros/as | **-amos** | **-imos** |
| vosotros/as | **-asteis** | **-isteis** |
| ellos/ellas/uds.| **-aron** | **-ieron** |

Note the accent marks on the "yo" and "él/ella/ud." forms. They are crucial for pronunciation and meaning.

Examples:
*   Ayer **hablé** con mi amigo. (Yesterday I spoke with my friend.)
*   Anoche **comimos** pizza. (Last night we ate pizza.)
*   La semana pasada, ellos **escribieron** un email. (Last week, they wrote an email.)
