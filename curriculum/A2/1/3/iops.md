
# Lesson 3: Indirect Object Pronouns

**New Words:**
*   me - to/for me
*   te - to/for you (informal)
*   le - to/for him, her, you (formal)
*   nos - to/for us
*   os - to/for you (plural, informal, Spain)
*   les - to/for them, you (plural)
*   dar - to give
*   decir - to say, to tell
*   escribir - to write

**Grammar:**
An indirect object is the person or thing that receives the direct object. It answers the question "to whom?" or "for whom?" the action is done. An indirect object pronoun (IOP) replaces this noun.

The forms for IOPs are the same as DOPs, except for the third person: **le** (singular) and **les** (plural).

Placement rules are the same as for DOPs (before a conjugated verb, or attached to an infinitive/participle).

Examples:
*   Yo **le** doy el libro a Juan. (I give the book to Juan.)
*   Ella **me** escribe una carta. (She writes a letter to me.)
*   **Te** digo la verdad. (I tell you the truth.)

**"A" Prepositional Phrase:**
Often, you will see the IOP used along with a prepositional phrase ("a" + noun) for clarity or emphasis.
*   **Le** compro flores **a mi madre**. (I buy flowers for my mom.)
