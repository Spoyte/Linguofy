# 🎵 Song: "Le Doy, Les Hablo" (I Give to Him/Her, I Speak to Them)

**Style:** Latin jazz with brass section - smooth and conversational rhythm that emphasizes giving and receiving actions

**Duration:** 2 minutes 55 seconds
