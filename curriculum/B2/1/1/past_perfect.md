
# Lesson 1: Past Perfect (Pluperfect)

**New Words:**
*   había, habías, había, habíamos, habíais, habían - imperfect of "haber"
*   ya - already
*   cuando - when
*   antes de que - before

**Grammar:**
The past perfect tense, also known as the pluperfect (*pluscuamperfecto*), is used to describe an action that happened **before another action in the past**. It is the past of the past.

If you have two past events, the past perfect describes the one that happened first. It is the equivalent of "I had done," "you had eaten," etc.

It is a compound tense, formed with the **imperfect** tense of the auxiliary verb **haber** + a **past participle**. (The past participles are the same ones you learned for the present perfect).

**Key Rule:** Just like the present perfect, the two parts of the verb, `haber` and the past participle, are **never separated**.

Examples:
*   Cuando llegué a la estación, el tren **ya se había ido**.
    *   (When I arrived at the station, the train **had already left**.)
    *   *Event 1: The train left. (Past Perfect)*
    *   *Event 2: I arrived. (Preterite)*

*   Ella no quiso ver la película porque **ya la había visto**.
    *   (She didn't want to see the movie because she **had already seen** it.)
    *   *Event 1: She saw the movie. (Past Perfect)*
    *   *Event 2: She didn't want to see it. (Preterite)*

*   No teníamos hambre porque **habíamos comido** antes de salir.
    *   (We weren't hungry because we **had eaten** before leaving.)
    *   *Event 1: We ate. (Past Perfect)*
    *   *Event 2: We weren't hungry. (Imperfect)*
