
# Lesson 4: Imperfect Subjunctive

**Grammar:**
The imperfect subjunctive is the past tense version of the subjunctive mood. It is used when the trigger verb in the main clause is in a past tense (preterite, imperfect, conditional, past perfect). It's used to talk about past wishes, doubts, and recommendations. It's most famously used in "if" clauses that describe hypothetical situations.

**Formation:**
1.  Take the **ellos/ellas/ustedes** form of the **preterite** tense (e.g., `hablaron`, `comieron`, `fueron`).
2.  **Drop** the "-ron".
3.  **Add** one of the two sets of endings. The "-ra" ending is more common.

| Pronoun | -ra Endings | -se Endings |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| yo | **-ra** | **-se** |
| tú | **-ras** | **-ses** |
| él/ella/ud. | **-ra** | **-se** |
| nosotros/as | **-ramos**| **-semos**|
| vosotros/as | **-rais** | **-seis** |
| ellos/ellas/uds.| **-ran** | **-sen** |

*Crucially, the `nosotros` form always has a written accent on the vowel before the ending (e.g., `habláramos`, `comiéramos`)*.

**Example Formation:**
*   hablar -> hablaron -> habla**ra** -> hablá**ramos**
*   comer -> comieron -> comie**ra** -> comié**ramos**
*   tener -> tuvieron -> tuvie**ra** -> tuvié**ramos**
*   ser/ir -> fueron -> fue**ra** -> fué**ramos**

**Main Uses:**

1.  **Hypothetical "If" Clauses:** Paired with the conditional tense.
    *   Si yo **tuviera** más dinero, viajaría por el mundo. (If I **had** more money, I would travel the world.)
    *   Si **fueras** a la fiesta, te divertirías. (If you **went** to the party, you would have fun.)

2.  **Past WEIRDO Triggers:** When the main clause is in the past.
    *   Esperaba que **vinieras**. (I hoped that you would come.)
    *   No creía que **fuera** posible. (I didn't believe it was possible.)
    *   Me pidió que lo **hiciera**. (He asked me to do it.)
