
# Lesson 6: Vocabulary - Emotions and Moods

**New Words:**
*   **Feliz / Contento(a):** Happy
*   **Triste:** Sad
*   **Enojado(a) / Enfadado(a):** Angry / Mad
*   **Sorprendido(a):** Surprised
*   **Asustado(a):** Scared / Frightened
*   **Nervioso(a):** Nervous
*   **Ansioso(a):** Anxious
*   **Tranquilo(a):** Calm / Quiet
*   **Cansado(a):** Tired
*   **Aburrido(a):** Bored
*   **Emocionado(a):** Excited
*   **Preocupado(a):** Worried
*   **Confundido(a):** Confused
*   **Avergonzado(a):** Ashamed / Embarrassed
*   **Orgulloso(a):** Proud
*   **Celoso(a):** Jealous
*   **Enamorado(a):** In love
*   **Decepcionado(a):** Disappointed
*   **Agobiado(a):** Overwhelmed
*   **Estresado(a):** Stressed

**Grammar Note:**
Most of these are adjectives, and almost all of them are used with the verb **estar**, because they describe a temporary state or condition.
*   *Hoy **estoy** muy emocionado.* (Today I am very excited.)
*   *Ella **estaba** preocupada por el examen.* (She was worried about the exam.)
