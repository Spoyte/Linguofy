
# Lesson 2: Future Perfect

**New Words:**
*   habré, habrás, habrá, habremos, habréis, habrán - future of "haber"
*   terminado - finished
*   graduado - graduated
*   para - by (referring to a deadline)
*   dentro de - within

**Grammar:**
The future perfect tense is used to talk about an action that **will have been completed** by a certain point in the future. It describes a future action that will be in the past relative to another future moment.

It is a compound tense, formed with the **future** tense of the auxiliary verb **haber** + a **past participle**.

(Remember, `haber` has an irregular stem in the future: `habr-`).

**Key Rule:** The two parts of the verb, `haber` and the past participle, are **never separated**.

Examples:
*   **Para las cinco**, ya **habré terminado** mi trabajo.
    *   (By five o'clock, I **will have already finished** my work.)
    *   *Future Point A: 5 o'clock. Future Point B: I finish my work. B happens before A.*

*   **Dentro de tres años**, mi hermano **se habrá graduado** de la universidad.
    *   (Within three years, my brother **will have graduated** from university.)

It can also be used to express **probability or conjecture about a past event**.
*   ¿Dónde está mi teléfono? - No sé, lo **habrás dejado** en el coche.
    *   (Where is my phone? - I don't know, you **probably left** it in the car.)
*   Mi amigo no ha llegado. **Se habrá perdido**.
    *   (My friend hasn't arrived. **He must have gotten** lost.)
