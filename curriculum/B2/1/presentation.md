
# Section 1: Perfecting the Past

Welcome to B2! This section is all about adding layers of complexity to your storytelling. You will learn the "perfect" tenses, which allow you to talk about actions that happened before other actions in the past, future, and conditional frames. We will also introduce the past subjunctive, which is essential for discussing hypothetical situations.

**Lessons in this section:**

*   **Lesson 1: Past Perfect (Pluperfect):** Learn to talk about what "had happened."
*   **Lesson 2: Future Perfect:** Learn to talk about what "will have happened."
*   **Lesson 3: Conditional Perfect:** Learn to talk about what "would have happened."
*   **Lesson 4: Imperfect Subjunctive:** Master the past subjunctive for hypothetical "if" clauses.
*   **Lesson 5: Past Perfect Subjunctive:** Combine the past perfect and the subjunctive to talk about impossible past conditions.
*   **Lesson 6: Vocabulary - Emotions and Moods:** Learn a wide range of adjectives to describe how you and others feel.
