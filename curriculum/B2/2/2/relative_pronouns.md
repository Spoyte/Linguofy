
# Lesson 2: Relative Pronouns

**Grammar:**
Relative pronouns (pronombre relativo) are words like "that," "which," and "who" that connect a main clause to a dependent (or relative) clause. They refer back to a noun (the antecedent) in the main clause.

**1. Que**
*   This is the most common relative pronoun. It can refer to **people or things**.
*   It can mean "that," "which," "who," or "whom."
*   Unlike in English, you can **never** omit "que" in Spanish.
    *   *El libro **que** leí era bueno.* (The book **that** I read was good.)
    *   *El hombre **que** trabaja aquí es mi amigo.* (The man **who** works here is my friend.)

**2. Quien / Quienes**
*   This pronoun refers **only to people**.
*   It is most often used after a preposition (like `a`, `con`, `de`, `en`) or in a non-restrictive clause (set off by commas).
*   It means "who" or "whom."
    *   *La chica **a quien** le di el libro es mi hermana.* (The girl **to whom** I gave the book is my sister.)
    *   *Mi jefe, **quien** es muy inteligente, habla tres idiomas.* (My boss, **who** is very intelligent, speaks three languages.)
    *   If there is no preposition, using `que` is more common: *El hombre **que** trabaja aquí...*

**3. El que / La que / Los que / Las que**
*   This means "the one who," "he who," or "the one that." It agrees in gender and number with the antecedent it replaces.
*   It is used to add clarity, especially after prepositions.
*   It can also be used to mean "the one(s) that" when the antecedent is omitted.
    *   *Hablé con la vecina de Juan, **la que** es abogada.* (I spoke with Juan's neighbor, the one who is a lawyer.) - *clarifies it's the neighbor, not Juan*
    *   ***Los que** estudian, aprueban.* (**Those who** study, pass.)

**4. Cuyo / Cuya / Cuyos / Cuyas**
*   This is a relative adjective meaning **"whose."**
*   It agrees in gender and number with the **noun it precedes** (the thing that is possessed), NOT with the owner.
    *   *Es el autor **cuyos** libros me encantan.* (He is the author **whose** books I love.) - `cuyos` agrees with `libros`.
    *   *Es la mujer **cuya** casa es azul.* (She is the woman **whose** house is blue.) - `cuya` agrees with `casa`.

**5. Donde**
*   This relative adverb means **"where"** and is used to refer to places.
    *   *La casa **donde** crecí ya no existe.* (The house **where** I grew up no longer exists.)
