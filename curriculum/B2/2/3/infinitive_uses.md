
# Lesson 3: Uses of the Infinitive

**Grammar:**
The infinitive is the basic, unconjugated form of a verb (`hablar`, `comer`, `vivir`). While it often translates to "to do" something, it has a variety of uses in Spanish.

**1. After a Conjugated Verb**
This is the most common use. When you have two verbs back-to-back and the subject is the same, the first verb is conjugated and the second remains an infinitive.
*   **Quiero comer.** (I want to eat.)
*   **Necesitamos hablar.** (We need to talk.)
*   **Suelen llegar** tarde. (They usually arrive late.)

**2. After a Preposition**
If a verb follows a preposition (a, de, con, en, para, por, sin, etc.), it is **always** in the infinitive form.
*   Gracias **por ayudar**. (Thanks for helping.)
*   **Antes de salir**, apaga la luz. (Before leaving, turn off the light.)
*   Estudio **para aprender**. (I study in order to learn.)
*   Sueño **con viajar** por el mundo. (I dream about traveling the world.)

**3. As a Noun**
The infinitive can act as the subject of a sentence. It is treated as a singular, masculine noun.
*   **Nadar** es mi pasatiempo favorito. (Swimming is my favorite hobby.)
*   **Ver** es creer. (Seeing is believing.)
*   No me gusta **madrugar**. (I don't like getting up early.)

**4. In Commands (for signs, recipes, etc.)**
For impersonal commands, like on a public sign or in a recipe, the infinitive is used.
*   **No fumar.** (No smoking.)
*   **Empujar.** (Push.)
*   **Mezclar** los ingredientes. (Mix the ingredients.)

**5. The structure "al + infinitivo"**
This special construction means "upon" or "when" doing something. It describes an action that immediately triggers another.
*   **Al llegar** a casa, vi que había un paquete. (**Upon arriving** home, I saw there was a package.)
*   **Al ver** a su madre, el niño sonrió. (**When he saw** his mother, the boy smiled.)
