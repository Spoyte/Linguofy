
# Section 2: Advanced Structures

In this section, you will learn to connect your ideas like a native speaker. We will cover the formal passive voice, master the pronouns that link clauses, and dive into the specific rules for a variety of advanced clausal structures, refining your understanding of when to use the indicative and when to use the subjunctive.

**Lessons in this section:**

*   **Lesson 1: The Passive Voice:** Learn the formal passive voice with the verb `ser`.
*   **Lesson 2: Relative Pronouns:** Master the use of `que`, `quien`, `el que`, and `cuyo`.
*   **Lesson 3: Uses of the Infinitive:** Explore the many ways the infinitive can function in a sentence.
*   **Lesson 4: Concessive Clauses:** Learn when to use the indicative vs. subjunctive with `aunque`.
*   **Lesson 5: Reported Speech:** Learn how to report what someone else said, including the "backshifting" of tenses.
*   **Lesson 6: Augmentatives and Diminutives:** Learn how to use suffixes like `-ito` and `-ón` to add nuance to your vocabulary.
