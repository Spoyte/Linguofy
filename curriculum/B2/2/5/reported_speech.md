
# Lesson 5: Reported Speech (Estilo Indirecto)

**Grammar:**
Reported speech (or indirect style) is how you report what someone else said without quoting them directly. When you do this in Spanish, you often have to change the tense of the verb from the original statement. This is called "backshifting".

The general rule is: **If the reporting verb is in the past (e.g., `dijo`, `explicó`, `preguntó`), the verb in the reported clause takes one step back into the past.**

**Tense Shift Rules:**

| Direct Speech Tense | -> | Indirect Speech Tense |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| Present | -> | Imperfect |
| Preterite | -> | Past Perfect (Pluperfect) |
| Present Perfect | -> | Past Perfect (Pluperfect) |
| Future | -> | Conditional |
| Present Subjunctive | -> | Imperfect Subjunctive |

**Examples:**

*   **Direct:** Juan: "Hoy **estoy** muy cansado." (I am very tired today.)
*   **Indirect:** Juan dijo que hoy **estaba** muy cansado. (Juan said that he was very tired today.)
    *   *Present -> Imperfect*

*   **Direct:** María: "**Fui** al cine ayer." (I went to the movies yesterday.)
*   **Indirect:** María me contó que **había ido** al cine el día anterior. (María told me that she had gone to the movies the day before.)
    *   *Preterite -> Past Perfect*

*   **Direct:** Carlos: "**He terminado** el proyecto." (I have finished the project.)
*   **Indirect:** Carlos anunció que **había terminado** el proyecto. (Carlos announced that he had finished the project.)
    *   *Present Perfect -> Past Perfect*

*   **Direct:** Ana: "**Iré** a la fiesta." (I will go to the party.)
*   **Indirect:** Ana prometió que **iría** a la fiesta. (Ana promised that she would go to the party.)
    *   *Future -> Conditional*

*   **Direct:** El profesor: "Espero que **estudiéis**." (I hope that you study.)
*   **Indirect:** El profesor dijo que esperaba que **estudiáramos**. (The professor said that he hoped that we would study.)
    *   *Present Subjunctive -> Imperfect Subjunctive*

**No Backshift:**
*   If the reporting verb is in the **present** tense (`dice que...`), no tense shift is needed.
*   If the statement is a **universal truth**, no tense shift is needed.
    *   *Direct:* "La Tierra **es** redonda."
    *   *Indirect:* Él explicó que la Tierra **es** redonda.
