
# Lesson 2: Plural "Ser"

**New Words:**
*   nosotros/nosotras somos - we are
*   vosotros/vosotras sois - you are (plural, informal, Spain)
*   ellos/ellas/ustedes son - they/you (plural) are

**Grammar:**
This lesson introduces the plural forms of the verb "ser".
*   **Nosotros somos** hombres. (We are men.)
*   **Vosotras sois** mujeres. (You [all] are women.)
*   **Ellos son** niños. (They are boys.)
*   **Ustedes son** niñas. (You [all] are girls.)

Note the gender agreement: "nosotros" for a male or mixed group, "nosotras" for an all-female group. The same applies to "vosotros/vosotras" and "ellos/ellas".
In Latin America, "ustedes" is used for all plural "you" (formal and informal), and "vosotros/as" is not used.
