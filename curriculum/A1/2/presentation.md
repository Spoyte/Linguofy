
# Section 2: Building Sentences

Now that you have the basics, this section will teach you how to expand your sentences. You'll learn how to talk about more than one thing by making nouns plural, and you'll conjugate your first set of action verbs for eating, drinking, and living.

**Lessons in this section:**

*   **Lesson 1: Plural Nouns:** Learn how to make nouns plural and use the articles `los` and `las`.
*   **Lesson 2: Plural "Ser":** Learn the plural forms of the verb "to be" (`somos`, `son`).
*   **Lesson 3: The Verb "Comer":** Conjugate your first regular `-er` verb, "to eat."
*   **Lesson 4: The Verb "Beber":** Practice `-er` verb endings with "to drink."
*   **Lesson 5: The Verb "Vivir":** Learn the pattern for regular `-ir` verbs with "to live."
