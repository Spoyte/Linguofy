
# Lesson 3: The Verb "Comer" (to eat)

**New Words:**
*   comer - to eat
*   yo como - I eat
*   tú comes - you eat
*   él/ella/usted come - he/she/you eat
*   manzana - apple
*   naranja - orange
*   pescado - fish
*   carne - meat

**Grammar:**
"Comer" is a regular "-er" verb. To conjugate it in the present tense, you drop the "-er" and add the following endings:
*   -o (for yo)
*   -es (for tú)
*   -e (for él/ella/usted)

Example:
*   Yo como una manzana. (I eat an apple.)
*   Tú comes pescado. (You eat fish.)
