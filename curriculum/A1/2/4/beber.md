
# Lesson 4: The Verb "Beber" (to drink)

**New Words:**
*   beber - to drink
*   yo bebo - I drink
*   tú bebes - you drink
*   él/ella/usted bebe - he/she/you drink
*   café - coffee
*   té - tea
*   jugo - juice

**Grammar:**
"Beber" is another regular "-er" verb, just like "comer". The conjugation pattern is the same.
*   -o (for yo)
*   -es (for tú)
*   -e (for él/ella/usted)

Example:
*   Yo bebo agua. (I drink water.)
*   Ella bebe leche. (She drinks milk.)
