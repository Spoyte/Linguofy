
# Lesson 5: The Verb "Vivir" (to live)

**New Words:**
*   vivir - to live
*   yo vivo - I live
*   tú vives - you live
*   él/ella/usted vive - he/she/you live
*   en - in
*   una casa - a house
*   un apartamento - an apartment

**Grammar:**
"Vivir" is a regular "-ir" verb. To conjugate it in the present tense, you drop the "-ir" and add these endings:
*   -o (for yo)
*   -es (for tú)
*   -e (for él/ella/usted)

This is very similar to "-er" verbs, but the "nosotros" and "vosotros" forms (which you'll learn later) are different.

Example:
*   Yo vivo en una casa. (I live in a house.)
*   Usted vive en un apartamento. (You live in an apartment.)
