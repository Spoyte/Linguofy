
# Lesson 2: Days of the Week

**New Words:**
*   lunes - Monday
*   martes - Tuesday
*   miércoles - Wednesday
*   jueves - Thursday
*   viernes - Friday
*   sábado - Saturday
*   domingo - Sunday
*   hoy - today
*   mañana - tomorrow

**Grammar:**
1.  **Not Capitalized:** Days of the week are not capitalized in Spanish.
2.  **Masculine:** All days of the week are masculine. You use the article "el" for a specific day and "los" for recurring days.
    *   **el** lunes - on Monday
    *   **los** lunes - on Mondays
3.  **The week starts on Monday.**

Example:
*   Hoy es **lunes**. (Today is Monday.)
*   Voy al parque **el sábado**. (I go to the park on Saturday.)
