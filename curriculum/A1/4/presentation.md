
# Section 4: Talking About the World

In this section, you'll learn vocabulary that helps you describe the world around you in more detail. We'll cover numbers and days of the week, and introduce two very important and unique Spanish verbs: "gustar" for liking things, and "estar" for talking about location and temporary states.

**Lessons in this section:**

*   **Lesson 1: Numbers 1-10:** Learn to count from one to ten.
*   **Lesson 2: Days of the Week:** Learn the names for the days of the week.
*   **Lesson 3: The Verb "Gustar":** Learn the unique structure for "to like."
*   **Lesson 4: "Hay" (There is/There are):** Learn the incredibly useful expression for existence.
*   **Lesson 5: The Verb "Estar":** Learn the second "to be" verb, used for location and temporary conditions.
*   **Lesson 6: Vocabulary - Places in the City:** Learn the names for common places in a town or city.
