
# Lesson 3: The Verb "Gustar" (to like)

**New Words:**
*   me gusta(n) - I like
*   te gusta(n) - you like
*   le gusta(n) - he/she/you like
*   el chocolate - chocolate
*   la música - music
*   los deportes - sports

**Grammar:**
The verb "gustar" works differently than most verbs. It literally means "to be pleasing to." The person who does the liking is the *indirect object*, not the subject.

1.  Use **gusta** if the thing you like is singular or is an infinitive verb.
2.  Use **gustan** if the thing you like is plural.
3.  Use the indirect object pronouns (me, te, le) to say *who* likes the thing.

Examples:
*   **Me gusta** el chocolate. (Chocolate is pleasing to me.)
*   **Te gusta** leer. (Reading is pleasing to you.)
*   **Le gustan** los deportes. (Sports are pleasing to him/her/you.)
