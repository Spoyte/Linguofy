
# Lesson 5: The Verb "Estar" (to be)

**New Words:**
*   estar - to be (location, temporary state)
*   yo estoy - I am
*   tú estás - you are
*   él/ella/usted está - he/she/you are
*   aquí - here
*   allí - there
*   cansado/a - tired
*   feliz - happy

**Grammar:**
Spanish has two verbs for "to be". You've learned "ser" for permanent characteristics. Now, meet "estar"!

Use **estar** for:
1.  **Location:** To say where something or someone is.
2.  **Temporary States & Feelings:** To describe how you are feeling or a temporary condition.

Notice the irregular "yo" form and the accent marks on "estás" and "está".

Examples:
*   Yo **estoy** aquí. (I am here.)
*   ¿Cómo **estás**? (How are you?) - a very common greeting!
*   Él **está** cansado. (He is tired.)
*   El café **está** caliente. (The coffee is hot.) - a temporary state.
