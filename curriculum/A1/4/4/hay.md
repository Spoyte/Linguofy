
# Lesson 4: "Hay" (There is/There are)

**New Words:**
*   hay - there is / there are
*   un problema - a problem
*   una pregunta - a question
*   mucho/mucha - a lot of, much
*   muchos/muchas - a lot of, many

**Grammar:**
"Hay" is the impersonal form of the verb "haber" and is used to express existence. It's incredibly useful!

1.  **It doesn't change:** It means both "there is" (singular) and "there are" (plural).
2.  **Use with indefinite articles:** You use "hay" with "un, una, unos, unas" (a, some) or with numbers, but *not* with definite articles "el, la, los, las" (the).

Examples:
*   **Hay** un libro en la mesa. (There is a book on the table.)
*   **Hay** tres coches en la calle. (There are three cars in the street.)
*   ¿**Hay** una pregunta? (Is there a question?)
*   No **hay** problema. (There is no problem.)
