
# Lesson 1: Numbers 1-10

**New Words:**
*   cero - zero
*   uno - one
*   dos - two
*   tres - three
*   cuatro - four
*   cinco - five
*   seis - six
*   siete - seven
*   ocho - eight
*   nueve - nine
*   diez - ten

**Grammar:**
The number "uno" changes to "un" before a masculine noun.
*   **un** libro (one book)
*   **una** mesa (one table)

You can use the verb "tener" to talk about age.
*   ¿Cuántos años tienes? - How old are you? (Literally: How many years do you have?)
*   Tengo **diez** años. - I am ten years old.
