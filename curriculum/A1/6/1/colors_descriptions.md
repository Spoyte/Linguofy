# Lesson 1: Colors and Basic Descriptions

**New Words - Colors:**
- rojo/roja - red
- azul - blue
- verde - green
- amarillo/amarilla - yellow
- negro/negra - black
- blanco/blanca - white
- gris - gray
- rosa - pink
- morado/morada - purple
- naranja - orange
- marrón - brown

**New Words - Basic Descriptions:**
- grande - big
- pequeño/pequeña - small
- nuevo/nueva - new
- viejo/vieja - old
- bonito/bonita - pretty/beautiful
- feo/fea - ugly
- alto/alta - tall/high
- bajo/baja - short/low
- largo/larga - long
- corto/corta - short

**Grammar:**
Colors and adjectives must agree with the gender and number of the noun they describe:

**Masculine singular:** El carro rojo (the red car)
**Feminine singular:** La casa roja (the red house)
**Masculine plural:** Los carros rojos (the red cars)
**Feminine plural:** Las casas rojas (the red houses)

**Note:** Some colors like azul, verde, gris, rosa, naranja, and marrón don't change for gender, only for number (plural adds -es).

**Practice Phrases:**
- Mi carro es azul - My car is blue
- La mesa pequeña - The small table
- Los libros verdes - The green books
- Las flores bonitas - The pretty flowers

**Real-World Usage:**
- Shopping: "¿Tienen esto en rojo?" (Do you have this in red?)
- Describing people: "Ella tiene ojos verdes" (She has green eyes)
- Giving directions: "La casa blanca grande" (The big white house)
