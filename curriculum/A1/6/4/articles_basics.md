# Lesson 4: Articles (un, una, el, la, los, las)

**New Words - Definite Articles (the):**
- el - the (masculine singular)
- la - the (feminine singular)  
- los - the (masculine plural)
- las - the (feminine plural)

**New Words - Indefinite Articles (a/an):**
- un - a/an (masculine)
- una - a/an (feminine)
- unos - some (masculine plural)
- unas - some (feminine plural)

**Grammar Rules:**

**Definite Articles:**
Use when talking about specific things or things in general:
- El libro está en la mesa - The book is on the table
- Me gusta la música - I like music (in general)

**Indefinite Articles:**
Use when talking about non-specific things:
- Necesito un lápiz - I need a pencil (any pencil)
- Hay una casa grande - There is a big house

**How to Know Masculine vs. Feminine:**

**Usually Masculine (-o endings):**
- el libro (book)
- el carro (car)
- el hermano (brother)

**Usually Feminine (-a endings):**
- la casa (house)
- la mesa (table)
- la hermana (sister)

**Exceptions to Remember:**
- el día (day) - masculine despite -a
- la mano (hand) - feminine despite -o
- el problema (problem) - masculine despite -a

**Practice Examples:**
- Un café, por favor - A coffee, please
- La profesora es muy buena - The teacher is very good
- Necesito unas plumas - I need some pens
- Los estudiantes estudian mucho - The students study a lot

**Common Mistakes to Avoid:**
- Don't say "la carro" ❌ → "el carro" ✅
- Don't say "un mesa" ❌ → "una mesa" ✅
- Remember: articles must match the noun's gender and number!
