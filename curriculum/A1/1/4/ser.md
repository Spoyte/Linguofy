
# Lesson 4: The Verb "Ser" (to be)

**New Words:**
*   yo soy - I am
*   tú eres - you are (informal)
*   él/ella/usted es - he/she/you (formal) are
*   un - a/an (masculine)
*   una - a/an (feminine)

**Grammar:**
This lesson introduces the verb "ser" (to be) for permanent characteristics or identities.

*   **Yo soy** un hombre. (I am a man.)
*   **Tú eres** una mujer. (You are a woman.)
*   **Él es** un niño. (He is a boy.)
*   **Ella es** una niña. (She is a girl.)
