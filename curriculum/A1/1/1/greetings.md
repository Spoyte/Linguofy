
# Lesson 1: Greetings

**New Words:**
*   Hola - Hello
*   Adiós - Goodbye
*   Gracias - Thank you
*   Por favor - Please
*   Sí - Yes
*   No - No

**Grammar:**
This lesson introduces basic vocabulary for greetings. There are no major grammar rules to learn at this stage, just memorizing the new words.
