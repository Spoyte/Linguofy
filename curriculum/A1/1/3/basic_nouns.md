
# Lesson 3: Basic Nouns

**New Words:**
*   el hombre - the man
*   la mujer - the woman
*   el niño - the boy
*   la niña - the girl
*   el pan - the bread
*   el agua - the water
*   la leche - the milk

**Grammar:**
In Spanish, nouns have a gender (masculine or feminine).
*   Masculine nouns often end in "-o" and use the article "el".
*   Feminine nouns often end in "-a" and use the article "la".
This is a general rule, and there are exceptions.
