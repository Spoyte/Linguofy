
# Lesson 5: Basic Adjectives

**New Words:**
*   bueno/buena - good
*   malo/mala - bad
*   grande - big
*   pequeño/pequeña - small
*   frío/fría - cold
*   caliente - hot

**Grammar:**
Adjectives in Spanish must agree in gender with the noun they modify.
*   If the noun is masculine, the adjective usually ends in "-o". (e.g., el pan bueno)
*   If the noun is feminine, the adjective usually ends in "-a". (e.g., la leche fría)
*   Some adjectives, like "grande" or "caliente", have the same form for both genders.
Adjectives usually come *after* the noun in Spanish.
