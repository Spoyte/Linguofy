# Section 1: The Absolute Basics

Welcome to Spanish! This first section will give you the essential building blocks for communication. We'll start with simple greetings, learn how to introduce yourself, and then dive into your first grammar topics. By the end of this section, you'll be able to form simple sentences and understand the core concept of gender in Spanish nouns.

**Lessons in this section:**

*   **Lesson 1: Greetings:** Learn how to say hello, goodbye, and other essential courtesies.
*   **Lesson 2: Introductions:** Ask someone their name and tell them yours.
*   **Lesson 3: Basic Nouns:** Discover your first nouns and the concept of masculine and feminine words.
*   **Lesson 4: The Verb "Ser":** Learn the most important verb in Spanish, "to be," for permanent states.
*   **Lesson 5: Basic Adjectives:** Learn how to describe things and make your adjectives agree with your nouns.
*   **Lesson 6: Cultural Context:** Learn about greeting customs across Spanish-speaking countries.
