
# Lesson 2: Introductions

**New Words:**
*   Me llamo - My name is
*   ¿Cómo te llamas? - What is your name?
*   Mucho gusto - Nice to meet you
*   Igualmente - Likewise
*   Señor - Mr.
*   Señora - Mrs.
*   Señorita - Miss

**Grammar:**
This lesson introduces the phrase "Me llamo" to state your name. It's a reflexive verb construction, but for now, you can just memorize the phrase.
