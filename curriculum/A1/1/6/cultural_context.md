# Lesson 6: Cultural Context - Greetings Across Spanish-Speaking Countries

**Cultural Notes:**

## How People Greet Each Other

In Spanish-speaking countries, greetings are often more physical and warm than in English-speaking countries:

**Common Greeting Customs:**
- **Spain**: Two kisses on the cheeks (starting with the right cheek)
- **Mexico**: One kiss on the cheek for women, handshake for men
- **Argentina**: One kiss on the cheek for everyone
- **Colombia**: One kiss on the cheek, or a hug among close friends

## Formal vs. Informal

**When to use "Hola":**
- With friends, family, and people your age
- In casual, relaxed situations
- When the other person uses "Hola" first

**When to use "Buenos días/tardes/noches":**
- With older people or authority figures
- In business or formal situations
- When entering a shop or restaurant
- When you're unsure of the level of formality

## Time-Based Greetings

**Buenos días** - Good morning (until about 2 PM)
**Buenas tardes** - Good afternoon/evening (2 PM - 8 PM)
**Buenas noches** - Good night (after 8 PM)

*Note: In some regions, people simply say "Buenas" as a shortened version.*

## Real-Life Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Entering a Small Shop**
- You: "Buenos días"
- Shopkeeper: "Buenos días, ¿cómo está usted?"
- You: "Bien, gracias"

**Scenario 2: Meeting a Friend**
- Friend: "¡Hola!"
- You: "¡Hola! ¿Cómo estás?"
- Friend: "Muy bien, ¿y tú?"

**Cultural Tip:** Always greet people when entering small spaces like elevators, waiting rooms, or small shops. It's considered polite!
