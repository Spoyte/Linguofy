
# Lesson 1: Possessive Adjectives

**New Words:**
*   mi/mis - my
*   tu/tus - your (informal)
*   su/sus - his/her/your (formal)/their
*   gato - cat
*   perro - dog
*   libro - book

**Grammar:**
Possessive adjectives show ownership. They must agree in *number* with the noun they modify (the thing that is owned).
*   Use "mi", "tu", "su" for singular nouns.
*   Use "mis", "tus", "sus" for plural nouns.

Examples:
*   **mi** perro (my dog) -> **mis** perros (my dogs)
*   **tu** libro (your book) -> **tus** libros (your books)
*   **su** gato (his/her/your cat) -> **sus** gatos (his/her/your cats)

"Su" and "sus" can be ambiguous. Context usually makes the meaning clear.
