
# Lesson 2: The Verb "Tener" (to have)

**New Words:**
*   tener - to have
*   yo tengo - I have
*   tú tienes - you have
*   él/ella/usted tiene - he/she/you have
*   hermano - brother
*   hermana - sister

**Grammar:**
"Tener" is an irregular verb. The "yo" form is unique ("tengo"), and the stem changes from "ten-" to "tien-" for "tú" and "él/ella/usted". This is a common pattern called a "stem-changing verb".

Examples:
*   Yo **tengo** un hermano. (I have a brother.)
*   Tú **tienes** una hermana. (You have a sister.)
*   Ella **tiene** un gato. (She has a cat.)
