
# Lesson 5: Question Words

**New Words:**
*   ¿Qué? - What?
*   ¿Quién? - Who?
*   ¿Cuándo? - When?
*   ¿Dónde? - Where?
*   ¿Por qué? - Why?
*   ¿Cómo? - How?

**Grammar:**
1.  **Punctuation:** Spanish uses an inverted question mark (¿) at the beginning of a question and a regular one at the end.
2.  **Word Order:** In simple questions, you can often just put the question word at the beginning of a statement.
    *   Statement: *Tú comes pan.* (You eat bread.)
    *   Question: *¿**Qué** comes?* (What do you eat?)
3.  **Accent Marks:** All question words have a written accent mark when used in a question.

Examples:
*   ¿**Qué** es? - What is it?
*   ¿**Quién** es él? - Who is he?
*   ¿**Dónde** vives? - Where do you live?
