
# Lesson 3: The Verb "Querer" (to want)

**New Words:**
*   querer - to want
*   yo quiero - I want
*   tú quieres - you want
*   él/ella/usted quiere - he/she/you want
*   un coche - a car
*   una bicicleta - a bicycle

**Grammar:**
"Querer" is another stem-changing verb, just like "tener". The stem changes from "quer-" to "quier-" in the same persons (yo, tú, él/ella/usted).

"Querer" can be followed by a noun (a thing you want) or an infinitive verb (an action you want to do).

Examples:
*   Yo **quiero** un coche. (I want a car.)
*   Tú **quieres** comer. (You want to eat.)
*   Él **quiere** beber agua. (He wants to drink water.)
