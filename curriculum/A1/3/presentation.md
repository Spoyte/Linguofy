
# Section 3: Expressing Yourself

This section introduces some of the most powerful verbs in Spanish. You'll learn how to talk about possession, wants, and movement. We'll also cover the essential vocabulary for asking questions, opening up a whole new dimension of communication.

**Lessons in this section:**

*   **Lesson 1: Possessive Adjectives:** Learn how to say "my," "your," and "his/her."
*   **Lesson 2: The Verb "Tener":** Learn the important irregular verb "to have."
*   **Lesson 3: The Verb "Querer":** Learn the stem-changing verb "to want."
*   **Lesson 4: The Verb "Ir":** Learn the highly irregular and crucial verb "to go."
*   **Lesson 5: Question Words:** Learn the basic words you need to ask questions, like "¿Qué?", "¿Dónde?", and "¿Quién?".
*   **Lesson 6: Vocabulary - The Family:** Learn the words for all the important members of your family.
