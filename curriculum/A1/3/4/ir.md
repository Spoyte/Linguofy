
# Lesson 4: The Verb "Ir" (to go)

**New Words:**
*   ir - to go
*   yo voy - I go
*   tú vas - you go
*   él/ella/usted va - he/she/you go
*   a - to
*   al (a + el) - to the (masculine)
*   la escuela - the school
*   el parque - the park

**Grammar:**
"Ir" is a highly irregular verb. Its conjugations don't follow a predictable pattern.

It is almost always followed by the preposition "a" (to).
When "a" is followed by the masculine article "el", they combine to form the contraction "al".
*   a + el = al

Examples:
*   Yo **voy al** parque. (I go to the park.)
*   Tú **vas a la** escuela. (You go to the school.)
*   Ella **va a** mi casa. (She goes to my house.)
