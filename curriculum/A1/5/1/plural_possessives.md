
# Lesson 1: Plural Possessive Adjectives

**New Words:**
*   nuestro/a/os/as - our
*   vuestro/a/os/as - your (plural, informal, Spain)
*   familia - family
*   amigos - friends
*   padres - parents

**Grammar:**
You've learned "mi, tu, su". Now for the plural owners.
Unlike "mi, tu, su", these adjectives must agree in both **gender and number** with the noun they modify (the thing being owned).

1.  **Nuestro** libro (our book)
2.  **Nuestra** casa (our house)
3.  **Nuestros** libros (our books)
4.  **Nuestras** casas (our houses)

The same pattern applies to "vuestro". The plural form of "su" remains "sus" for all plural owners (they, you all).

Examples:
*   **Nuestra** familia es grande. (Our family is big.)
*   **Vuestros** amigos son simpáticos. (Your friends are nice.)
*   **Sus** padres están aquí. (Their/Your parents are here.)
