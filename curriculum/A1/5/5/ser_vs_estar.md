
# Lesson 5: Review - Ser vs. Estar

This lesson is a review of the two verbs "to be". There are no new words.

**Grammar Summary:**

Use **SER** for things that are more permanent, essential characteristics. A good way to remember is the acronym **DOCTOR**:
*   **D**escription: *Yo soy alto.* (I am tall.)
*   **O**ccupation: *Tú eres estudiante.* (You are a student.)
*   **C**haracteristic: *Ella es inteligente.* (She is intelligent.)
*   **T**ime: *Son las tres.* (It is three o'clock.)
*   **O**rigin: *Somos de España.* (We are from Spain.)
*   **R**elationship: *Él es mi hermano.* (He is my brother.)

Use **ESTAR** for temporary conditions and location. A good way to remember is the acronym **PLACE**:
*   **P**osition: *El libro está en la mesa.* (The book is on the table.)
*   **L**ocation: *Yo estoy en casa.* (I am at home.)
*   **A**ction: *Estamos hablando.* (We are talking.) - *You'll learn this tense later!*
*   **C**ondition: *Tú estás cansado.* (You are tired.)
*   **E**motion: *Ella está feliz.* (She is happy.)

**Key Contrast:**
*   *La manzana **es** verde.* (The apple is green - its type).
*   *La manzana **está** verde.* (The apple is unripe - its condition).
