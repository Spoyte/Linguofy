
# Lesson 2: Numbers 11-20

**New Words:**
*   once - eleven
*   doce - twelve
*   trece - thirteen
*   catorce - fourteen
*   quince - fifteen
*   dieciséis - sixteen
*   diecisiete - seventeen
*   dieciocho - eighteen
*   diecinueve - nineteen
*   veinte - twenty

**Grammar:**
From 16 to 19, the numbers are formed by combining "diez y" (ten and) into a single word.
*   diez y seis -> dieciséis
*   diez y siete -> diecisiete

Notice the accent mark on "dieciséis" to maintain the correct stress.
From 20 onwards, you'll see a new pattern, which you will learn in a future lesson.

Example:
*   Hay **quince** estudiantes en la clase. (There are fifteen students in the class.)
*   Mi número favorito es el **veinte**. (My favorite number is twenty.)
