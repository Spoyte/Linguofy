
# Section 5: Putting It All Together

In this final section of the A1 level, we'll build on your existing knowledge to add more detail and precision to your Spanish. You'll learn the rest of the possessive adjectives, expand your counting skills, and learn to talk about dates and time. We'll finish with a crucial review comparing the two "to be" verbs, `ser` and `estar`.

**Lessons in this section:**

*   **Lesson 1: Plural Possessive Adjectives:** Learn how to say "our" and "your" (plural).
*   **Lesson 2: Numbers 11-20:** Continue learning to count.
*   **Lesson 3: Months of the Year:** Learn the months and how to give dates.
*   **Lesson 4: Telling Time:** Learn the basics of asking for and telling the time.
*   **Lesson 5: Review - Ser vs. Estar:** Solidify your understanding of the two "to be" verbs.
*   **Lesson 6: Vocabulary - Food and Drink:** Learn essential words for different foods and meals.
