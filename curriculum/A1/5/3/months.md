
# Lesson 3: Months of the Year

**New Words:**
*   enero - January
*   febrero - February
*   marzo - March
*   abril - April
*   mayo - May
*   junio - June
*   julio - July
*   agosto - August
*   septiembre - September
*   octubre - October
*   noviembre - November
*   diciembre - December

**Grammar:**
1.  **Not Capitalized:** Just like the days of the week, months are not capitalized in Spanish.
2.  **Masculine:** All months are masculine, but you don't usually use an article ("el") with them unless you're referring to a specific instance (e.g., "el diciembre pasado" - last December).
3.  **To state a date:** Use the format: *el [day] de [month]*
    *   *el cinco de mayo* - the fifth of May
    *   *el primero de enero* - the first of January (use "primero" for the first of the month)

Example:
*   Mi cumpleaños es en **agosto**. (My birthday is in August.)
*   La Navidad es en **diciembre**. (Christmas is in December.)
