
# Lesson 2: Future Tense (Irregulars)

**Grammar:**
While the *endings* for the future tense are always regular, some verbs have an irregular **stem**. Instead of using the full infinitive, you use the irregular stem and add the same future endings you already know.

There are three main groups of irregular stems:

1.  **-d- group:** The final vowel of the infinitive is replaced by a `-d-`.
    *   tener -> **tendr-** (yo **tendré** - I will have)
    *   poner -> **pondr-** (tú **pondrás** - you will put)
    *   salir -> **saldr-** (él **saldrá** - he will leave)
    *   venir -> **vendr-** (nosotros **vendremos** - we will come)
    *   valer -> **valdr-** (vosotros **valdréis** - you will be worth)

2.  **"ec" removal group:** The "-ec-" is removed from the infinitive.
    *   hacer -> **har-** (yo **haré** - I will do/make)
    *   decir -> **dir-** (tú **dirás** - you will say/tell)

3.  **"e" removal group:** The "e" is removed from the `-er` ending.
    *   haber -> **habr-** (él **habrá** - there will be)
    *   poder -> **podr-** (nosotros **podremos** - we will be able to)
    *   querer -> **querr-** (vosotros **querréis** - you will want)
    *   saber -> **sabr-** (ellos **sabrán** - they will know)

Examples:
*   No **tendré** tiempo mañana. (I will not have time tomorrow.)
*   ¿Qué **harás** este fin de semana? (What will you do this weekend?)
*   **Podremos** ir al cine. (We will be able to go to the movies.)
