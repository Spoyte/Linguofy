
# Lesson 5: Present Perfect

**New Words:**
*   he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, han - present tense of "haber"
*   hablado - spoken
*   comido - eaten
*   vivido - lived
*   hecho - done/made (irregular participle)
*   visto - seen (irregular participle)
*   escrito - written (irregular participle)
*   dicho - said/told (irregular participle)
*   ya - already
*   todavía no - not yet
*   alguna vez - ever (have you ever...?)

**Grammar:**
The present perfect tense describes actions that have happened in the recent past and have a connection to the present, or actions that happened at an unspecified time in the past. It's the equivalent of "I have done," "you have eaten," etc.

It is a compound tense, formed with the present tense of the auxiliary verb **haber** + a **past participle**.

To form the past participle:
*   For **-ar** verbs, drop the ending and add **-ado**. (hablar -> hablado)
*   For **-er** and **-ir** verbs, drop the ending and add **-ido**. (comer -> comido, vivir -> vivido)
*   Many common verbs have irregular past participles (e.g., hacer -> hecho, ver -> visto).

**Key Rule:** The two parts of the verb, `haber` and the past participle, are **never separated**. Pronouns and the word "no" always go before `haber`.

Examples:
*   Yo **he comido** paella muchas veces. (I have eaten paella many times.)
*   ¿**Has visto** esa película? (Have you seen that movie?)
*   Ellos **no han terminado** la tarea todavía. (They have not finished the homework yet.)
*   **Ya lo he hecho**. (I have already done it.)
