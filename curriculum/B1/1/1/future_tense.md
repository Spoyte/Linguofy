
# Lesson 1: The Future Tense

**New Words:**
*   hablaré - I will speak
*   comeré - I will eat
*   viviré - I will live
*   mañana - tomorrow
*   la próxima semana - next week
*   el próximo año - next year

**Grammar:**
The simple future tense describes what *will happen* or what someone *will do* in the future. In Spanish, it's a single word, and the great news is that the endings are the same for all verbs, **-ar, -er, and -ir**!

To form it, you take the **entire infinitive** and add the following endings:

| Pronoun | Endings |
| :--- | :---: |
| yo | **-é** |
| tú | **-ás** |
| él/ella/ud. | **-á** |
| nosotros/as | **-emos**|
| vosotros/as | **-éis** |
| ellos/ellas/uds.| **-án** |

All endings have a written accent mark except for the `nosotros` form.

Examples:
*   Mañana **hablaré** con mi jefe. (Tomorrow I will speak with my boss.)
*   **Comeremos** en un restaurante esta noche. (We will eat at a restaurant tonight.)
*   ¿**Viajarán** ustedes a México el próximo año? (Will you all travel to Mexico next year?)
