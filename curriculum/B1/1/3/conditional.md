
# Lesson 3: The Conditional Tense

**New Words:**
*   hablaría - I would speak
*   comería - I would eat
*   viviría - I would live
*   me gustaría - I would like
*   deberías - you should
*   si - if

**Grammar:**
The conditional tense is used to talk about what *would* happen, or what someone *would do* under certain conditions. It's also used to make polite requests and give advice.

The great news is that it's formed almost exactly like the future tense!
1.  The endings are the same for **-ar, -er, and -ir** verbs.
2.  The stems are the **exact same** as the future tense stems (including all the irregulars!).

To form it, you take the **infinitive** (or the irregular future stem) and add the following endings, which are the same as the `-er`/`-ir` endings in the imperfect.

| Pronoun | Endings |
| :--- | :---: |
| yo | **-ía** |
| tú | **-ías** |
| él/ella/ud. | **-ía** |
| nosotros/as | **-íamos**|
| vosotros/as | **-íais** |
| ellos/ellas/uds.| **-ían** |

Examples:
*   Si tuviera dinero, **compraría** un coche. (If I had money, I **would buy** a car.) - *Hypothetical*
*   **Me gustaría** un café, por favor. (I **would like** a coffee, please.) - *Polite Request*
*   Yo que tú, **hablaría** con él. (If I were you, I **would speak** with him.) - *Advice*
*   ¿**Podrías** ayudarme? (**Could** you help me?) - *Polite Request*
*   Yo **diría** que es una mala idea. (I **would say** it's a bad idea.) - *Opinion*
