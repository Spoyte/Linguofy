
# Lesson 4: Por vs. Para

"Por" and "para" can both mean "for", but they are not interchangeable. This is one of the most challenging topics for Spanish learners.

**Grammar Summary:**

**PARA (Destination / Purpose)**
Think of **Para** as pointing forward, towards a **D**eadline, **D**estination, or **G**oal.
*   **P**urpose (in order to): *Estudio **para** aprender.* (I study **in order to** learn.)
*   **E**ffect / Goal: *Trabajo mucho **para** ganar dinero.* (I work a lot **to** earn money.)
*   **R**ecipient: *Este regalo es **para** ti.* (This gift is **for** you.)
*   **F**uture / Deadline: *La tarea es **para** el lunes.* (The homework is **for** Monday.)
*   **E**mployment: *Trabajo **para** Google.* (I work **for** Google.)
*   **C**omparison / Opinion: ***Para** mí, el español es fácil.* (**For** me, Spanish is easy.)
*   **T**owards a Place: *Voy **para** el centro.* (I'm going **towards** downtown.)
(Mnemonic: **PERFECT**)

**POR (Cause / Motion)**
Think of **Por** as movement **through** time and space. It deals with the **R**eason or **C**ause **for** an action.
*   **A**round a place / Motion: *Caminamos **por** el parque.* (We walked **through/around** the park.)
*   **T**hrough a place: *Paso **por** tu casa a las ocho.* (I'll pass **by** your house at 8.)
*   **R**eason / Motive: *Lo hice **por** amor.* (I did it **for** love.)
*   **A**fter (going to get something): *Voy al supermercado **por** leche.* (I'm going to the store **for** milk.)
*   **C**ost / Price: *Pagué veinte dólares **por** el libro.* (I paid $20 **for** the book.)
*   **T**hanks / Exchange: *Gracias **por** la ayuda.* (Thanks **for** the help.)
*   **E**xchange / "per": *Cien kilómetros **por** hora.* (100 km **per** hour.)
*   **D**uration of time: *Estudié **por** dos horas.* (I studied **for** two hours.)
(Mnemonic: **ATTRACTED**)

Examples:
*   Voy a la tienda **por** pan. (Reason/Motive)
*   Este pan es **para** la cena. (Purpose/Goal)
*   Viajamos **por** dos semanas. (Duration)
*   Salimos **para** Madrid mañana. (Destination)
