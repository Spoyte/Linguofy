
# Section 1: New Tenses and Old Foes

Welcome to the intermediate level! This section will equip you with two brand-new tenses to talk about the future and hypothetical situations. You'll learn the future tense ("will do") and the conditional tense ("would do"). We will also tackle one of the most famously difficult topics in Spanish—the difference between `por` and `para`—and introduce your first compound tense.

**Lessons in this section:**

*   **Lesson 1: The Future Tense:** Learn how to talk about what will happen.
*   **Lesson 2: Future Tense (Irregulars):** Master the irregular stems for the future tense.
*   **Lesson 3: The Conditional Tense:** Learn how to talk about what would happen.
*   **Lesson 4: Por vs. Para:** A deep dive into the different uses of these two prepositions.
*   **Lesson 5: Present Perfect:** Learn your first compound tense, "I have done."
