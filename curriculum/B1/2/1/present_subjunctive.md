
# Lesson 1: Present Subjunctive (Regular Verbs)

**New Words:**
*   que yo hable - that I speak
*   que tú comas - that you eat
*   que él viva - that he live
*   Espero que... - I hope that...
*   Quiero que... - I want that...
*   Es importante que... - It's important that...

**Grammar:**
The subjunctive is not a tense, but a **mood**. It's used to express things that are not necessarily facts, but rather wishes, desires, doubts, emotions, or impersonal expressions. It's almost always found in a dependent clause introduced by the word "que".

**Formation (The "Yo-Drop-Swap" Rule):**
1.  Take the **yo** form of the verb in the present indicative (e.g., `hablo`, `como`, `vivo`).
2.  **Drop** the "-o".
3.  **Swap** the ending:
    *   For **-ar** verbs, add **-e, -es, -e, -emos, -éis, -en**.
    *   For **-er** and **-ir** verbs, add **-a, -as, -a, -amos, -áis, -an**.

| Pronoun | -ar (hable) | -er (coma) | -ir (viva) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| yo | habl**e** | com**a** | viv**a** |
| tú | habl**es**| com**as** | viv**as** |
| él/ella/ud. | habl**e** | com**a** | viv**a** |
| nosotros/as | habl**emos**| com**amos**| viv**amos**|
| vosotros/as | habl**éis**| com**áis** | viv**áis** |
| ellos/ellas/uds.| habl**en** | com**an** | viv**an** |

The subjunctive is triggered by certain verbs/phrases in the main clause. The key is a **change of subject** between the main clause and the dependent clause.

Examples (using the trigger **W**ishes):
*   Yo quiero ir. (No subjunctive - no change of subject).
*   Yo **quiero que** tú **vayas**. (I want *you to go*. Change of subject -> subjunctive).
*   **Espero que** ella **tenga** un buen día. (I hope *that she has* a good day).
*   **Es importante que** nosotros **estudiemos**. (It's important *that we study*).
