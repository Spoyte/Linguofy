
# Section 2: The Subjunctive Mood

This section introduces the single most important concept in intermediate-to-advanced Spanish: the subjunctive mood. You will learn how to form the present subjunctive and, more importantly, *when* to use it. This mood is used to express wishes, emotions, doubts, and other non-factual situations. Mastering the subjunctive is the key to unlocking fluency.

**Lessons in this section:**

*   **Lesson 1: Present Subjunctive (Regular Verbs):** Learn the "yo-drop-swap" rule for forming the subjunctive.
*   **Lesson 2: Present Subjunctive (Irregulars):** Cover stem-changing verbs and the famous "DISHES" irregulars.
*   **Lesson 3: Subjunctive Triggers (WEIRDO):** Use a mnemonic to learn the main reasons to use the subjunctive.
*   **Lesson 4: Formal Commands (Usted/Ustedes):** See how the subjunctive is used to give formal commands.
*   **Lesson 5: Informal "Tú" Commands:** Learn how to form affirmative and negative commands for friends and family.
