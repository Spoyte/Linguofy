
# Lesson 3: Subjunctive Triggers (WEIRDO)

**Grammar:**
Now that you know how to *form* the subjunctive, let's learn when to *use* it. The mnemonic **WEIRDO** covers the main categories of subjunctive triggers. Remember, these phrases in the main clause will trigger the subjunctive in the dependent clause (after "que") when there is a change of subject.

*   **W**ishes & Wants: Verbs expressing desire.
    *   *querer, esperar, desear, preferir, necesitar, ojalá*
    *   **Quiero que** me llames. (I want you to call me.)
    *   **Ojalá que** no llueva. (I hope it doesn't rain.)

*   **E**motions: Verbs expressing feelings.
    *   *alegrarse de, sentir, sorprender, tener miedo de, gustar*
    *   **Me alegro de que** estés aquí. (I'm happy that you're here.)
    *   **Siento que** no puedas venir. (I'm sorry that you can't come.)

*   **I**mpersonal Expressions: Phrases that state an opinion or judgment.
    *   *es importante, es necesario, es bueno/malo, es una lástima, es mejor*
    *   **Es necesario que** estudies mucho. (It's necessary that you study a lot.)
    *   **Es una lástima que** se vaya. (It's a shame that he's leaving.)
    *   **Exception:** If the expression states a fact or certainty (es verdad que, es obvio que, es cierto que), you use the **indicative**.

*   **R**ecommendations & Requests: Verbs of command or suggestion.
    *   *recomendar, sugerir, pedir, decir, mandar, prohibir*
    *   **Te recomiendo que** pruebes el pulpo. (I recommend that you try the octopus.)
    *   El profesor **pide que** hagamos la tarea. (The teacher asks that we do the homework.)

*   **D**oubt & Denial: Verbs or phrases expressing uncertainty or disbelief.
    *   *dudar, no creer, no pensar, no estar seguro de, negar*
    *   **Dudo que** él tenga la respuesta. (I doubt that he has the answer.)
    *   **No creo que** sea posible. (I don't believe it's possible.)

*   **O**rdering & Ojalá: This category overlaps with Wishes, covering commands and the specific phrase "Ojalá".
    *   *This is often just included to make the acronym work, but reinforces the command and strong wish aspect.*
