
# Lesson 2: Other Temporal Conjunctions

**Grammar:**
The rule you learned for `cuando` (using the subjunctive for a pending/future action) also applies to a number of other conjunctions that introduce a time-dependent clause.

These conjunctions require the **subjunctive** when the action in the subordinate clause has not yet occurred at the time of speaking.

**Conjunctions that follow the `cuando` rule:**

*   **En cuanto** (as soon as)
*   **Tan pronto como** (as soon as)
*   **Después de que** (after)
*   **Hasta que** (until)
*   **Mientras** (while, as long as)

**Examples:**

*   **En cuanto llegue** el paquete, te avisaré. (As soon as the package arrives, I will let you know.)
    *   *Compare to the indicative for a past event: En cuanto llegó el paquete, le avisé.*

*   **Tan pronto como sepa** la noticia, te la diré. (As soon as I know the news, I will tell you.)
    *   *Compare to the indicative for a habitual event: Tan pronto como sabe algo, me lo dice.*

*   Saldremos **después de que deje** de llover. (We will leave after it stops raining.)
    *   *Note: If there is no change of subject, you can use `después de + infinitivo`: Saldremos después de comer.*

*   No me iré **hasta que me pagues**. (I will not leave until you pay me.)
    *   *The payment is a future, pending event.*

*   Te ayudaré **mientras tenga** tiempo. (I will help you as long as I have time.)
    *   *This implies a future condition. If it were a statement of fact, you would use the indicative: "Te ayudo mientras tengo tiempo" (I help you while I have time).*

**A Special Case: `Antes de que`**
The conjunction `antes de que` (before) is **always** followed by the **subjunctive**, because the action it introduces has, by definition, not yet happened relative to the main clause.
*   Llama a tu madre **antes de que sea** demasiado tarde. (Call your mother before it's too late.)
*   Terminé el proyecto **antes de que me lo pidiera** mi jefe. (I finished the project before my boss asked me for it.) -> *even in the past, the asking had not yet happened at the time of finishing.*
