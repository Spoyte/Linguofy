
# Section 2: Mastering the Subjunctive

This final section is dedicated to achieving mastery over the subjunctive mood by applying it to complex clausal structures. You will learn how the subjunctive is used in temporal, adjective, and adverbial clauses depending on the certainty of the information. We will conclude with a deep dive into the powerful expressions made possible with the word `ojalá`.

**Lessons in this section:**

*   **Lesson 1: Temporal Clauses with "Cuando":** Learn when to use the subjunctive after "when."
*   **Lesson 2: Other Temporal Conjunctions:** Apply the `cuando` rule to other conjunctions like `hasta que` and `en cuanto`.
*   **Lesson 3: Adjective Clauses:** Use the subjunctive to talk about things that are indefinite or may not exist.
*   **Lesson 4: Adverbial Clauses:** Use mnemonics to master a wide range of adverbial conjunctions and their subjunctive triggers.
*   **Lesson 5: Expressing "If Only" with Ojalá:** Use `ojalá` with different past tenses to express wishes and regrets.
