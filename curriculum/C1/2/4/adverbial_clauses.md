
# Lesson 4: Adverbial Clauses

**Grammar:**
Adverbial clauses are clauses that function as adverbs, modifying the main verb by providing information about time, purpose, condition, or concession. You have already seen many of these (temporal, conditional, concessive). The key is to know which conjunctions always take the subjunctive and which can take either indicative or subjunctive.

We can group the conjunctions into two categories based on the acronyms **ESCAPA** and **THE CD**.

**1. ESCAPA (Always Subjunctive)**
Conjunctions that express a pending action, purpose, or provision. The action in the subordinate clause has not yet happened from the perspective of the main clause, so they are **always** followed by the **subjunctive**.

*   **E**n caso de que - in case
*   **S**in que - without
*   **C**on tal de que - provided that, as long as
*   **A**ntes de que - before
*   **P**ara que - so that, in order that
*   **A** menos que - unless

Examples:
*   Te doy un mapa **en caso de que te pierdas**. (I'll give you a map in case you get lost.)
*   Entró **sin que nadie lo viera**. (He came in without anyone seeing him.)
*   Te presto dinero **con tal de que me lo devuelvas**. (I'll lend you money provided that you pay me back.)
*   Termina tu trabajo **antes de que salgas**. (Finish your work before you leave.)
*   Estudio mucho **para que mis padres estén** orgullosos. (I study a lot so that my parents are proud.)
*   No saldremos **a menos que deje** de llover. (We won't go out unless it stops raining.)

**2. THE CD (Indicative or Subjunctive)**
This group includes the temporal and concessive conjunctions you've seen. They take the **subjunctive** if the action is pending/hypothetical, and the **indicative** if it is factual/habitual.

*   **T**an pronto como
*   **H**asta que
*   **E**n cuanto
*   **C**uando
*   **D**espués de que
*   (`Aunque` is also in this group)

Examples:
*   **En cuanto llegue**, llámame. (Subjunctive - future/pending)
*   **En cuanto llegó**, me llamó. (Indicative - past/factual)

*   No me iré **hasta que termines**. (Subjunctive - future/pending)
*   No me fui **hasta que terminó**. (Indicative - past/factual)
