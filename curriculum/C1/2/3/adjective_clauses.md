
# Lesson 3: Adjective Clauses

**Grammar:**
An adjective clause is a type of relative clause that describes a noun (the antecedent). In Spanish, the verb in an adjective clause can be in the **indicative** or the **subjunctive**.

The choice depends on whether the antecedent is **known and specific** or **unknown and indefinite**.

**1. Indicative in Adjective Clauses**
Use the indicative when the antecedent (the noun being described) is **known, definite, and specific** to the speaker. You are talking about someone or something whose existence and identity are certain.

*   Busco al mecánico que **habla** alemán. (I'm looking for the mechanic who speaks German.)
    *   *Antecedent: `al mecánico` (specific). I know this mechanic exists. The clause describes a known person.*
*   Tengo un amigo que **vive** en Madrid. (I have a friend who lives in Madrid.)
    *   *Antecedent: `un amigo` (specific). He is a real, known person.*
*   Quiero comprar el coche que **tiene** el motor V8. (I want to buy the car that has the V8 engine.)
    *   *Antecedent: `el coche` (specific). I have a particular car in mind.*

**2. Subjunctive in Adjective Clauses**
Use the subjunctive when the antecedent is **unknown, indefinite, or nonexistent**. You are looking for someone or something that may or may not exist, or you are describing a quality that you want this person/thing to have.

This is often triggered by verbs like `buscar`, `querer`, `necesitar`, or negative statements like `no hay nadie que...`

*   Busco un mecánico que **hable** alemán. (I'm looking for a mechanic who speaks German.)
    *   *Antecedent: `un mecánico` (indefinite). I don't know if such a mechanic exists, but this is the quality I'm looking for.*
*   Necesito un amigo que **viva** en Madrid. (I need a friend who lives in Madrid.)
    *   *Antecedent: `un amigo` (indefinite). I am looking for anyone who fits this description.*
*   ¿Hay alguien aquí que **pueda** ayudarme? (Is there anyone here who can help me?)
    *   *Antecedent: `alguien` (indefinite).*
*   No hay nadie que **lo sepa** todo. (There is no one who knows everything.)
    *   *Antecedent: `nadie` (nonexistent).*
*   Quiero un coche que **tenga** un motor V8. (I want a car that has a V8 engine.)
    *   *Antecedent: `un coche` (indefinite). Any car, as long as it meets the condition.*
