
# Lesson 2: Verbal Periphrasis

**Grammar:**
Verbal periphrasis is a construction that uses two or more verbs to create a single, nuanced verbal idea. It typically consists of:
`conjugated verb + (preposition/conjunction) + infinitive/gerund/participle`

These constructions express obligation, intention, repetition, the beginning, duration, or end of an action.

**Common Periphrasis Structures:**

**1. Obligation:**
*   **Tener que + infinitivo:** (to have to do something - strong obligation)
    *   *Tengo que terminar este informe.*
*   **Deber + infinitivo:** (should, ought to do something - moral obligation)
    *   *Debes respetar a tus mayores.*
*   **Hay que + infinitivo:** (one must do something - impersonal obligation)
    *   *Hay que comprar pan.*

**2. Start of an Action:**
*   **Echar(se) a + infinitivo:** (to start suddenly doing something, often with verbs of motion or emotion)
    *   *El niño se echó a llorar.* (The boy burst out crying.)
*   **Ponerse a + infinitivo:** (to start/get down to doing something)
    *   *Me puse a limpiar la casa.* (I started cleaning the house.)
*   **Empezar/Comenzar a + infinitivo:** (to begin to do something)
    *   *Empezó a llover.* (It started to rain.)

**3. Duration or Process of an Action:**
*   **Estar + gerundio:** (to be doing something - action in progress)
    *   *Estoy leyendo.*
*   **Seguir/Continuar + gerundio:** (to keep on doing something)
    *   *Sigo pensando en el problema.* (I keep thinking about the problem.)
*   **Andar + gerundio:** (to go around doing something)
    *   *Anda diciendo mentiras.* (He's going around telling lies.)
*   **Llevar + time + gerundio:** (to have been doing something for a certain time)
    *   *Llevo tres años estudiando español.* (I have been studying Spanish for three years.)

**4. End of an Action:**
*   **Dejar de + infinitivo:** (to stop doing something)
    *   *Dejó de fumar el año pasado.* (He stopped smoking last year.)
*   **Acabar de + infinitivo:** (to have just done something)
    *   *Acabo de llegar a casa.* (I have just arrived home.)
*   **Terminar de + infinitivo:** (to finish doing something)
    *   *Cuando termines de comer, lava los platos.* (When you finish eating, wash the dishes.)

**5. Repetition:**
*   **Volver a + infinitivo:** (to do something again)
    *   *He vuelto a leer mi libro favorito.* (I have read my favorite book again.)
