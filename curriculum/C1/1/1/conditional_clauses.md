
# Lesson 1: Conditional Clauses ("Si" Clauses)

**Grammar:**
Conditional sentences, or "si" clauses, express a condition and its consequence. There are three main types, based on whether the condition is likely, unlikely, or impossible.

**Type 1: Likely or Factual Conditions**
This is used for real, possible, or likely situations. It describes what will happen if a certain condition is met.

*   **Structure:** `Si + presente de indicativo, ... futuro / presente / imperativo`
*   **Meaning:** If X happens, Y will happen / happens / do Y.

Examples:
*   Si **llueve** mañana, no **saldré**. (If it rains tomorrow, I won't go out.)
*   Si **tienes** tiempo, **ayúdame**, por favor. (If you have time, help me, please.)
*   Si el agua **hierve**, **se evapora**. (If water boils, it evaporates.)

**Type 2: Unlikely or Hypothetical Conditions**
This is used for situations that are contrary to fact in the present or unlikely to happen in the future.

*   **Structure:** `Si + imperfecto de subjuntivo, ... condicional simple`
*   **Meaning:** If X were true / were to happen, Y would happen.

Examples:
*   Si **fuera** rico, **compraría** un yate. (If I were rich, I would buy a yacht.)
    *   *Reality: I am not rich.*
*   Si **tuvieras** más tiempo, **harías** más ejercicio. (If you had more time, you would exercise more.)
    *   *Reality: You don't have more time.*

**Type 3: Impossible Past Conditions**
This is used for situations that were contrary to fact in the past. It talks about how things *would have been* different if a past condition had been different. It is often used to express regret.

*   **Structure:** `Si + pluscuamperfecto de subjuntivo, ... condicional perfecto`
*   **Meaning:** If X had happened, Y would have happened.

Examples:
*   Si **hubiera sabido** que venías, te **habría preparado** la cena. (If I had known you were coming, I would have prepared dinner for you.)
    *   *Reality: I didn't know, so I didn't prepare dinner.*
*   No **habríamos perdido** el tren si **hubiéramos salido** antes. (We would not have missed the train if we had left earlier.)
    *   *Reality: We didn't leave earlier, so we missed the train.*
