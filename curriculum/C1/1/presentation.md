
# Section 1: Near-Native Nuances

Welcome to C1! At this advanced stage, the focus shifts from learning new grammar to mastering the nuances of what you already know. This section consolidates your knowledge of conditional "si" clauses, explores complex verbal structures, and introduces the discourse markers that will make your speech flow naturally. We'll also look at an obscure literary tense and refine your use of articles.

**Lessons in this section:**

*   **Lesson 1: Conditional Clauses ("Si" Clauses):** A comprehensive review of all three types of "if" clauses.
*   **Lesson 2: Verbal Periphrasis:** Master common verbal constructions like `dejar de`, `volver a`, and `echar a`.
*   **Lesson 3: Discourse Markers:** Learn the glue that holds conversations together (`sin embargo`, `por lo tanto`, etc.).
*   **Lesson 4: Preterite Perfect:** Learn to recognize a rare, literary past tense.
*   **Lesson 5: Advanced Uses of the Definite Article:** Master the use of `el`, `la`, `los`, `las` in ways that differ from English.
