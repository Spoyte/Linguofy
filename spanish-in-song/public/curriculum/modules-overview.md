# Spanish in Song – Curriculum Overview

Below you’ll find a concise guide to the six modules.  For each chapter you get:
• A one-sentence theme summary  
• Key grammar targets  
• Mini-lesson tips you can read before listening to the songs.

---

## Module 1 – Premiers Contacts et l’Essence de l’Être
**Theme:** Greetings, identity, polite words.  
**Grammar Focus:** ser (soy, eres, es) • pronouns yo / tú • gender of nouns.  
**Mini-Lesson**  
1. Spanish has two articles for “the”: **el** (masc.) and **la** (fem.).  
2. The verb **ser** links to permanent identity. _Yo soy Marta._  
3. Stress the **j** like a soft French “r”: _¡Hola!_ → /x/.

---

## Module 2 – Mon Quotidien en Deux Langues
**Theme:** Daily routine, family, food.  
**Grammar Focus:** present tense regular verbs (-ar, ‑er, ‑ir) • **estar** for location • gustar.  
**Mini-Lesson**  
1. To say “I like”, flip the logic: **Me gusta el café** (the coffee pleases _me_).  
2. **Estoy en casa** – use **estar** for where you are, **ser** for what something is.  
3. Verb endings: ‑o, ‑as, ‑a (-ar); ‑o, ‑es, ‑e (-er/-ir).

---

## Module 3 – L’Aventure du Voyage
**Theme:** Asking directions, hotel Spanish, travel culture.  
**Grammar Focus:** futur proche (_voy a + inf._) • por vs para • ser vs estar in locations.  
**Mini-Lesson**  
1. **por** = route/cause ( _Paso por el parque_ ), **para** = destination/purpose ( _Voy para la playa_ ).  
2. **La habitación es grande** (permanent), but **la ventana está abierta** (temporary).  
3. _Perdone, ¿cómo llego…?_ is a safe formula for any ask-for-directions moment.

---

## Module 4 – Cœur, Esprit et Faux Amis
**Theme:** Feelings, opinions, infamous false friends.  
**Grammar Focus:** creer que / pensar que + indicatif • por / para review • common false cognates.  
**Mini-Lesson**  
1. **embarazada ≠ embarrassed**; it means “pregnant”! The adjective for embarrassed is **avergonzado/a**.  
2. After **creo que** use indicative: _Creo que es verdad._  
3. **Por** expresses cause: _Gracias por tu ayuda._  **Para** expresses goal: _Estudio para viajar._

---

## Module 5 – Chroniques du Passé
**Theme:** Story-telling in the past, nostalgia.  
**Grammar Focus:** pretérito indefinido vs imperfecto.  
**Mini-Lesson**  
1. **Indefinido** = action chain: _Ayer salí, comí, bailé._  
2. **Imperfecto** = background: _La música sonaba, la gente reía._  
3. Try the movie vs photo trick—indefinido moves the movie, imperfecto paints the photo.

---

## Module 6 – Le Monde des Possibles
**Theme:** Wishes, orders, hypotheses (subjunctive).  
**Grammar Focus:** present subjunctive triggers (ojalá, quiero que) • imperfect subj. in Si-clauses.  
**Mini-Lesson**  
1. **Ojalá que + subj.** = “May … / Hopefully …”. _Ojalá que venga._  
2. With two subjects you need **que** + subj.: _Quiero que vengas._  
3. Unreal present: _Si yo fuera rico, viajaría por el mundo._
